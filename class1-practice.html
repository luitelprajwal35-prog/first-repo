Study Tips

Study in short blocks of 25 minutes, then take a five minute break. Your brain keeps information better when it gets regular rest.

Explain what you learned out loud, as if you were teaching a friend. If you get stuck, that is the exact part to review again.

Keep your phone in another room while you study. Even a silent phone on the desk pulls your attention away.

Write down three things you want to finish before you sit down. A small, clear list is easier to start than a vague plan.

Sleep matters more than one extra hour of cramming. Memory is stored while you rest.

When you finish a task, tick it off. Seeing progress is a real motivator.
